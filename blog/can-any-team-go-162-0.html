Can any baseball team go 162-0? We simulated it

BaseRogue Blog · 2026-11-18

The best team in history won 116 games. We built rosters of legends and played full seasons to find out what a perfect season really takes.

The record nobody has touched

The most wins in a 162-game season is 116, by the 2001 Seattle Mariners (the 1906 Chicago Cubs also won 116 in a 154-game schedule). The best winning percentage of the modern era belongs to those Cubs at .763. A 162-0 season would mean winning about 31% more often than the best team that ever lived. Even great teams lose about one game in three, because in baseball the best hitter fails seven times out of ten and any pitcher can have a bad night.

So we tried it

BaseRogue's All-Time League is a full 162-game season against a 32-team league, simulated plate appearance by plate appearance from real player ratings. We ran the same experiment three ways:

The all-time team was Babe Ruth, Willie Mays, Hank Aaron, Honus Wagner, Rogers Hornsby, Jimmie Foxx, Tris Speaker, Alex Rodriguez and Willie Wells in the field, a rotation of Walter Johnson, Randy Johnson, Pedro Martinez, Clayton Kershaw and Greg Maddux, and Mariano Rivera closing. Against a league of good but ordinary all-time rosters, it went 162-0 twice in five tries, and its worst season was 160-2.

What it takes

Three things decide whether a team can run the table. First, no weak spots: the drop from a superstar to a merely good player at even one position is enough to lose a handful of games. Second, a rotation where the fifth starter is as good as most teams' ace, because over 162 games he pitches more than 30 times. Third, depth: starters get tired, hitters need days off, and the bench plays more than you think. In our runs, a team of real stars from one era (the Tigers and Indians example) still lost 40 or more games a season.

FAQ

Has any MLB team gone 162-0?
No. The record is 116 wins, by the 2001 Seattle Mariners in 162 games and the 1906 Chicago Cubs in 154.
What is the 162-0 challenge?
A fan challenge: build a team of all-time players and see if it can win all 162 games of a season. BaseRogue's All-Time League plays the full season, game by game, against a 32-team league.
Can a team of the greatest players ever go 162-0?
In our simulations, a team with the best card at every position went 162-0 in two out of five seasons and never lost more than two games.
