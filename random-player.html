How the cards are rated

Every player in BaseRogue is rated from 1 to 125 in six skills (contact, power, eye, strikeout avoidance, speed and defense for hitters; hits, strikeouts, walks, home runs, stamina and clutch for pitchers), built from his best seasons and adjusted for the era he played in. The back of each card shows his peak seasons as a 162-game average.

Like what you pulled? In the game you open packs like this one to build a team, then play a full season against a league of all-time players.
