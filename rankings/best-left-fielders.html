How these are ranked

Players are listed at their main position. Every player in BaseRogue is rated from 1 to 125 in six skills, built from his best seven seasons (mixed with his best three and best fifteen) and adjusted for the era he played in, so a deadball star and a Statcast slugger sit on the same scale. The list uses each card's overall rating (OVR).
