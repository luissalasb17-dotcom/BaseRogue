Who ruled baseball from 1920 to 1941? The ten best hitters and the ten best pitchers of the Golden Era, with their cards.

Tap a card to flip it and see the peak-season stats on the back.

How these are ranked

Players belong to the era of their peak seasons (1920-1941). Every player in BaseRogue is rated from 1 to 125 in six skills, built from his best seven seasons (mixed with his best three and best fifteen) and adjusted for the era he played in, so a deadball star and a Statcast slugger sit on the same scale. The list uses each card's overall rating (OVR).
