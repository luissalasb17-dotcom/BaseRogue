The All-Time League: build a team of legends and play a full 162-game season

Babe Ruth, Josh Gibson, Pedro Martinez and Shohei Ohtani in the same league. Build a 25-man roster of real players from 1871 to today, play every game of a 32-team season and see if you can go 162-0. Free, in your browser.

Start a season

Three ways to build your team

Hobby Box: open five packs of 15 real player cards (Wax, Retail, Hobby, Premium and Mystery packs, each with its own odds) and keep five from each, with three rerolls. Snake draft: pick the eras that exist this season, then draft 25 rounds against 31 rival general managers from one shared pool. All-Star Dream Team: take every card you have unlocked and build the team you always wanted.

Chemistry rewards players from the same franchise, era or country, captains lift their teammates, and up to three Iron Men play almost every day.

A real league, 162 games

Your team joins a 32-team league split into the American and National Leagues. Every game is simulated plate appearance by plate appearance: starters tire according to their Stamina, closers protect leads, relievers need rest, the bench gets platoon starts and pinch-hit at-bats, and each player's line follows his card. A manager AI moves hot bench bats into the lineup and reorders the rotation month by month.

The season screen reads like a newspaper: a calendar of all 162 games, a pennant-race chart, Pythagorean record, playoff odds and magic number, monthly reports, a trade deadline and The BaseRogue Gazette with the story of the day.

Awards, All-Stars and October

Writers vote an MVP, Cy Young, Reliever of the Year, Silver Sluggers and Gold Gloves in each league; fans vote the All-Star rosters at midseason and an All-MLB Team closes the year. The top four of each league make the playoffs, and October is played on the dice table: you roll every at-bat of your series with the real odds of each matchup.

Keep it going: franchise mode

When the season ends, protect ten players, open a new box or draft again and start the next year. Season goals reward you with better packs or a higher draft pick, and the league history book keeps every champion and award winner.

FAQ

What is the All-Time League?
BaseRogue's full season mode: build a 25-man roster of real players from every era and play all 162 games plus the playoffs against a league built from the same pool of all-time players.
Can you go 162-0?
That is the challenge. The best real record is 116 wins. With a great roster you can chase a perfect season, but every rival is built from the same legends.
Where do the ratings come from?
From 150 years of real statistics (the Lahman database and Baseball-Reference WAR), adjusted for the era each player played in, so a deadball-era star and a modern slugger can be compared on the same card.
Is it free?
Yes. Like the rest of BaseRogue it is free, with no account and no download.
